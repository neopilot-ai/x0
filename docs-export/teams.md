# Teams

Collaborate with your team on v0 projects.

## Invite Members

Use the **Invite** button in the chat header to open collaboration settings:

- Invite individual team members
- Manage view and edit permissions
- Share chats with your team

## Team Features

- Shared projects
- Role-based access control
- Activity feeds and notifications
